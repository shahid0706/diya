Diya (दीया): privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Diya is a free learning app for children. It has no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Diya does not collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on the phone

A child's first name or nickname, chosen picture, age group, language, lesson progress and stars are saved only on the device, so the child can carry on where they stopped. Deleting the app or using "Remove this learner" in Settings deletes them.

A grown-up can choose to record their own voice for praise, numbers and first words (Settings, "Record a voice", behind a grown-ups-only question). The app asks for microphone permission only when the grown-up taps Record. The recordings are stored only on that device and are never uploaded.

Internet

The app works fully offline. The app store versions do not connect to the internet for lessons and do not include any chat with an AI or with other people.

Children

Diya is made for children and follows the App Store Kids category and Google Play Families rules: no ads, no third-party analytics, no data collection, and no links out of the app without a grown-up check.
